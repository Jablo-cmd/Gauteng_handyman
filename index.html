Quick answers

Before you get in touch.

Which areas do you cover?

The business advertises services around Johannesburg and the East Rand. Contact the team to confirm availability in your suburb.

Can I ask about several repairs at once?

Yes. Include the list of jobs in your message so the team can understand the scope of the work.

Does this form calculate my quote?

No. It prepares an enquiry for WhatsApp. Pricing must be confirmed directly by the business after reviewing your requirements.

What information should I include?

Your suburb, a short description of the issue, the type of property and any relevant photos can help explain the job.
