A few quick answers

Before you get in touch.

Which areas do you cover?

The business advertises services across Johannesburg and surrounding areas, including the East Rand. Contact the team to confirm availability for your suburb.

Can I ask about more than one job?

Yes. Include the list of repairs or improvements in your enquiry so the team can understand the scope.

Can you give me a price from this form?

This form prepares an enquiry message; it does not calculate a quote. The team will need to review your requirements and confirm pricing directly.

What should I include in my message?

Your suburb, a short description of the issue, the type of property and any relevant photos can help the team understand the job.
